## The question

Series Three is **the inside tests**. Series One asked what the models
do from the outside, on real clips. Series Two ran the council's locked
battery. Series Three asks: **is the loss in the file, or inside the
model, and if inside, exactly where?**

It came from two questions of Kobby's (Nov 3, 2026):

1. If a codec compresses sound lossily, a quiet sound might be dropped
   before the model ever hears it. Then a miss could be the file's
   fault, not the model's.
2. What if the model compresses on purpose, inside itself? To answer
   that for open models, we can open the model and trace it.

Rules of the lane: tracing works only on open models (Pegasus stays
behavioral forever); a checker result never convicts alone (the
scrambled control must sit at chance; surgery is the causal check);
predictions are written down before each run.

## How a series ends and the next one begins

A series is one question, not one model and not one instrument.

- **A series closes** when its question is answered as far as its own
  tests can answer it, its claims ledger below is settled (every claim
  standing, narrowed, or withdrawn on the record), and its remaining
  items are either done or formally handed to the next series in this
  file.
- **A new series opens** the day its question is named. It gets its own
  journey file on this same pattern, opened with a handoff section:
  what the previous series settled, what it hands over, and what the
  new question is. Kobby names each series, as he named this one.
- Series Three closes when the inside tests are done on the second
  model (MiniCPM: tracing, then the codec control) and the fixpack's
  answers are in the ledger. What Series Four's question is, is not
  decided yet; nothing in this file assumes it.
