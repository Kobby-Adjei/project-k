# The both-matched naming control: read (Nov 4, 2026)

The last blocker from the council's freeze audits. Every
earlier open-form contrast crossed a different property in
the control arm than in the real arm, so "crossing pitch
transplants the donor's naming" had no true no-effect
control. This run adds it: a donor matched on BOTH
properties (same pitch band, same onset band, different
instance). One notebook, one model load, all contrasts
within-run. Data: namingcontrol_openform.csv (66 rows:
18 unpatched, 24 real, 24 bothmatched), verified against
the run log; token check passed; exit 0, no errors.

## The setup

Six low recipients, the fixpack's set: three early-band
(unpatched answers all "silent"), three late-band
(unpatched: "a brief beep followed by silence", "a busy
signal", "a telephone dial tone"). Each recipient gets two
pastes at layers 14, 19, 24, 28: real (donor high, same
onset band) and bothmatched (donor low, same onset band,
different instance). The real donors all answer "beep"
unpatched, 6 of 6. The matched donors answer like the
recipients' own family: "silent" for the early three,
"a busy signal" for the late three.

## The result

Real pastes: the recipients answer "beep", the donor's
exact word, in 24 of 24 cells (6 of 6 at every layer,
14 through 28).

Both-matched pastes: 0 of 24 cells produce the donor-style
bare "beep". The answers stay in the recipients' own
texture: "silent" for the early three at every layer; for
the late three, busy-signal phrasings throughout, and the
first recipient gives "a brief beep followed by a dial
tone" (layers 14-24) and "a beep sound" (layer 28) — its
own answer family ("a brief beep followed by silence"
unpatched), perturbed in wording, never the transplant.

## What it settles

The naming transplant is specific to crossing pitch. A
paste in itself does not do it: the same machinery, the
same layers, a donor differing on neither property leaves
the recipient naming as it and its donor already do,
while the pitch-crossing donor overrides all six
recipients to its own word at every tested layer. The
naming claim in the evidence ledger moves to established,
stated as a transplant (the donors name it unpatched
anyway), not a rescue.
