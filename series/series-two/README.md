# Series Two: which stage loses it

The council battery. Where Series One asks whether the
sound is used, Series Two asks where it is lost: a 2x2 of
sound alone versus sound with a picture, crossed with open,
presence, and detail question forms; a forced yes or no
battery with matching and lure targets and muted controls,
scored where the model exposes a yes score; and a tone
timing ladder, one tone placed at 2, 6, and 10 seconds.

- The battery as designed is 87 asks per model; its design
  record is summarized in method/METHOD.md.
- The four *-diagnostics-summary.md files: the read for
  each open model.
- The three *-equal-diagnostics-summary.md files: the
  reads of the Series One equal battery, run in the same
  sessions.
- The CSVs: the raw result files for every run in this
  folder, both batteries, all four open models.
- feed-audit-minicpm.md: the audit that proved the earlier
  silent-padding fault and set the per-ask feed proof rule.
- pegasus-yesno/: the Pegasus text version of the yes or no
  battery, and its current status.

Status: complete for all five models. Four open models,
four different places where the sound is lost. The Pegasus
text version of the yes or no battery finished Nov 5, 2026;
see pegasus-yesno/STATUS.md.
