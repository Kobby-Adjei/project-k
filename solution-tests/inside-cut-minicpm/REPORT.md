Vacuum (0.0759 still, 0.0953 gray), speech (0.0953), and the mixed vacuum (0.0141) sit firmly on the No side, below every silence ask except one. Silence asked about sizzling scores 0.6225, above the real sizzle's own 0.4378. The wrong-sound lures all score near zero (0.0086 to 0.2227): the score is specific, it is the present vacuum/speech/mixed cases that rank low.
- What it showed: Path B is KILLED on MiniCPM under the bar frozen before the run. Scope: one model, 18 asks, audio-alone 4-second cuts, the presence form only.
