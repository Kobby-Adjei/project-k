# Kill test: tagger hint (sound turned into text) on Pegasus 1.6
- Date: Nov 12, 2026 (design frozen before the run; Kobby's go Nov 11 to 12). 8 new Pegasus calls, all HTTP 200, temperature 0, via custom.twelvelabs-temp4. Design: kill-test-wrapper/DESIGN-FROZEN-TAGGER.md.
- Tagger: CLAP (laion/clap-htsat-unfused), pretrained, zero-shot, used as-is. Candidates frozen: "running water", "a vacuum cleaner", "applause and clapping", "silence". It heard each clip's full audio track (16 kHz mono).
- What came back (scored from tagger_results.json and tagger_hint_results.csv, same synonym rules as the earlier kill tests):
  - Arm T, tagger alone: 4 of 4. Water scored 0.9884 on "running water", vacuum 0.9059, applause 1.0000, and the silence clip scored 0.9615 on "silence".
  - Arm M, model alone (reused from the frozen Path A kill test, same clips, same standard prompt, temperature 0): 0 of 3, silence clean.
  - Arm H, model + the tagger's true label in the prompt: 3 of 3. Water named, vacuum named, applause named. Silence, with the hint "no distinct sound", named no sound and closed "there are no sounds detected in the video, making it entirely silent."
  - Arm L, model + a deliberately wrong label: blind copies 4 of 4. The water clip "became" a vacuum cleaner, the vacuum "became" applause and clapping (with invented context about an audience), the applause "became" running water, and the silent clip, told "running water", reported "the sound of running water, which can be heard in the background." The answers are near-identical templates with only the sound name swapped.
- Verdict under the bar frozen before the run: the path FAILS the kill test. Conditions 1 and 2 passed outright (hint names at least 2 of 3; silence hint clean), but condition 3 failed completely: blind copies 4 of 4 against a maximum of 1, and the silence lure named water, which the design said fails the path on the spot. What the test actually proves is narrower and sharper than a fix: Pegasus repeats the note it is given, right or wrong, and checks nothing. With a true note its answers become correct (3 of 3); with a false note it invents the sound (4 of 4), including on silence. The working system here is the tagger's ears with Pegasus as a loudspeaker: any public recipe would have to credit the tagger for the hearing and disclose that the video model adds no verification. Scope: one model, 3 sounds, 1 repeat per cell; the tagger's own 4 of 4 is on these same 4 clips and is itself a pilot result.
- Source files: ~/workspace/project-k-pilot/kill-test-wrapper/ (DESIGN-FROZEN-TAGGER.md, tagger_results.json, tagger_hint_results.csv, raw_tagger/, run_tagger.py, run_tagger_hint_test.py).
